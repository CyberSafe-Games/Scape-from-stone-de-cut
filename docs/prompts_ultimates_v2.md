# Prompts das ilustrações de ultimate

Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada. Imagens novas, fundo opaco, uma chamada
por ilustração. Referências de estilo visíveis na conversa: carta Machados Gêmeos,
carta antiga Rugido da Floresta e ilustrações geradas na mesma série.
As molduras e os textos finais são compostos pelo jogo.

## Mana Ancestral

Create a new square pixel-art illustration for the ultimate skill MANA ANCESTRAL in a Brazilian folklore dark fantasy deckbuilding game. Reference images in this conversation show the game's pixel-art warrior and card style: preserve the recognizable young tan/brown-skinned warrior with black hair, black headband with red-and-white feathers, brown tribal loincloth and gold wrist ornaments. Depict the warrior at the center raising both hands and channeling a colossal brilliant sapphire/cyan ancestral energy sphere, three smaller spectral orbs orbiting it, luminous ancient stone runes, cyan lightning, mist and starlike sparks. He is empowered and majestic, with a confident focused expression. Rich dark teal jungle temple background, dramatic bright blue rim lighting, finely hand-placed pixel-art shading and crisp dark outlines. Make it feel considerably more powerful and imposing than a normal card. Composition must read clearly as a small card illustration: one main character, strong central silhouette, large readable magical shapes. Artwork only, full bleed, no card frame, no words, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. Square composition. Save the resulting image as a local output file.

## Forças Ancestrais

Create a square pixel-art dark fantasy card illustration for the ultimate FORÇAS ANCESTRAIS, matching the MANA ANCESTRAL illustration just generated and this game's native pixel-art warrior. Same recognizable tan/brown-skinned young male warrior, black hair and red-and-white feather headband, brown tribal loincloth, gold wrist ornaments. A low-angle imposing heroic portrait, warrior centered with clenched fists and arms spread, burning golden eyes, an enormous golden amber ancestral guardian towering behind him, broad luminous spectral arms wrapping around him as protective armor. A sculpted ancestral wooden mask floats above the warrior, golden runic circles, chunks of levitating stone and powerful amber rays radiating outward. Dark jungle ruins, blackened bronze, vivid gold and warm ivory magical highlights, limited emerald accents, ornate yet clearly readable silhouette. This is a legendary ultimate, much more imposing than normal skills. Crisp pixel-art edges, deep shaded textures, carefully placed pixels. Main character and spirit clearly visible at small card-art size. Artwork only, full bleed, NO frame, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO watermark. Square composition.

## Rugido da Floresta

Create the third square pixel-art dark fantasy illustration in the same ultimate-card series as the blue MANA ANCESTRAL and golden FORÇAS ANCESTRAIS just generated. Skill: RUGIDO DA FLORESTA. Same identifiable young tan/brown-skinned warrior with black hair, black/red headband with red and white feathers, brown tribal loincloth and gold arm ornaments. He stands centered, chest thrust forward, fists clenched, unleashing a fierce supernatural roar. An enormous majestic emerald-green spectral jaguar head erupts above and behind him with its jaws open in a thunderous roar, gigantic golden fangs, luminous eyes. Expanding concentric green and gold sonic shockwaves shatter ancient stone shields and blackened armor fragments in the foreground. Swirling tropical leaves, brilliant emerald spectral smoke, jagged green rays and gold sparks through a dark moonlit jungle. A commanding, legendary ultimate that breaks enemy defenses. Strong readable silhouette at small card illustration size. Dense hand-placed pixel-art textures, crisp dark outlines, dramatic high contrast, rich emerald, jade, gold and midnight teal. Artwork only, full bleed, NO card frame, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO watermark. Square composition.
